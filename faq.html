Frequently Asked Questions

AI and Society in Action I

Q

The prerequisite for AIE3905 is: AIE1001-1002, CSC1001-1002, CSC1003-1004, or CSC1005-1006 (or equivalents demonstrating baseline programming literacy). Are students who do not satisfy the prerequisite requirements eligible to apply for the course?

A

For applicants who have not fully satisfied the prerequisite requirements, we generally expect them to have strong self-learning ability and to have already gained some programming experience through self-study or equivalent learning experiences.

If you are admitted to the course, the prerequisite requirement may be waived. The teaching team will review all applications and make section assignments based on the overall application pool. Interviews may also be arranged if necessary.

The final admission decision will be made by the teaching team. We encourage you to carefully consider your academic background and study plan before submitting your application.

Q

If I am admitted to the course but later discover a timetable conflict, can I withdraw from the course?

A

The class schedule for each section is available on the course website. Please review it carefully together with your required course timetable before applying. In general, students are not permitted to withdraw from the course after they have been admitted and enrolled.

Q

Is there an estimated timeframe for the field trip for each section?

A

The field trip will be arranged during the Fall Term and will not require travel outside the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area. The exact schedule will be determined and announced by each teaching section based on its course plan.

Q

Is AIE3905 a traditional lecture course, or does it follow AIE4902's model of team project work?

A

Actually, neither. The course outline available on the official webpage provides a detailed breakdown of the course structure and expectations.

Q

When submitting my resume for this course, should I emphasize social/practical experience or technical background?

A

Try putting yourself in the instructor's shoes: what would you want to see in a student's resume?

Q

How difficult is this course, and how heavy is the workload?

A

It varies from person to person. The perceived difficulty and workload depend heavily on your background and level of effort—what might be challenging for one student could be relatively manageable for another.

Q

What kind of corporate collaboration projects are specifically involved in AIE3905 S03? Are these projects fixed in advance, or can they be chosen based on interests?

A

In this course, students are expected to conduct research and actively participate in defining the project scope—this is a more advanced skill set. For detailed information on the course content and project arrangements, please refer to the course syllabus, which provides a comprehensive overview.
